Privacy & Safety

Data protection

Images and patient details are stored locally in the browser (localStorage) for this prototype. No cloud upload occurs in demo mode. In production, data would be encrypted in transit (TLS) and at rest, with role-based access and audit logs.

Not a diagnostic device

VisionCare AI is a screening and decision-support tool. It does not provide a final diagnosis or prescribe treatment. All referral decisions must be confirmed by a qualified ophthalmologist.

Consent & de-identification

Health workers must obtain verbal/written consent before capture. EXIF is stripped before analysis. Patient IDs are pseudonymised (VC-YYYY-XXXX) and reports can be printed without phone numbers.

Limitations

Performance depends on image quality and may be lower for media opacities, small pupils, or other pathologies. The model is demonstrated on simulated data; clinical validation and regulatory approval would be required before deployment.
